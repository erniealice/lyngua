# Asset Details

View comprehensive details of a fixed asset, including cost, depreciation, book value, and maintenance history.

## What you can do here

- View asset name, category, location, and current status
- See acquisition cost, purchase date, and depreciation method
- Track accumulated depreciation and current book value
- View depreciation schedule showing monthly or annual depreciation expense
- Record maintenance and repair costs
- Update asset location or assignment
- Retire or dispose of the asset and record any gain or loss

## Common workflows

**To record maintenance:** Click Add Maintenance, enter the date, description, and cost, then save.

**To retire an asset:** Click Retire Asset, confirm the retirement date and reason, then record any gain or loss on disposal.

**To review depreciation:** View the Depreciation tab to see the full schedule of depreciation expenses posted to date.

## Tips

- Book value (cost minus accumulated depreciation) is used for balance sheet reporting
- Maintenance costs increase the asset's efficiency but do not alter its depreciable base
- Keep detailed maintenance records to support asset condition assessments and resale decisions
