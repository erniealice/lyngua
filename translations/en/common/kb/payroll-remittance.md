# Payroll Remittances

Track and manage remittance of payroll taxes, employee benefits, and other withholdings to government agencies and third parties.

## What you can do here

- View all payroll remittances organized by type (federal tax, state tax, FICA, workers comp, benefits)
- See remittance schedule by payment deadline
- Record remittance payments with date, amount, and confirmation
- Track remittance status (due, pending, paid, overdue)
- View remittance history and aging
- Generate remittance reports for audit and compliance
- Set up remittance alerts for upcoming deadlines

## Common workflows

**To record a tax remittance:** Click Add Remittance, select the remittance type, enter the payment date and amount, and confirm the submission.

**To view upcoming remittances:** Use the calendar or list view to see all remittances due in the coming weeks.

**To verify payment:** Confirm the remittance payment with the agency or financial institution and update status to "Paid".

## Tips

- Timely remittance of payroll taxes and contributions is a legal requirement
- Tracking remittances helps ensure compliance and identifies any late payments before penalties apply
- Remittance records support audits and payroll expense verification
