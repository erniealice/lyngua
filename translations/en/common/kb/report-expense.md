# Expense Report

Analyze operating expenses by category, department, or business unit to control costs and improve profitability.

## What you can do here

- View operating expenses organized by category (salaries, rent, utilities, marketing, etc.)
- See expense trends over time and period-over-period comparisons
- Filter expenses by department, cost center, or business unit
- View expenses as a percentage of revenue
- Identify high-expense areas and cost drivers
- Compare actual spending to budget or forecast
- Export expense data for departmental review and cost control

## Common workflows

**To review period expenses:** Select a time period to see all operating expenses and major cost categories.

**To analyze by department:** Filter by department or cost center to see spending allocation and identify overspending.

**To track cost control:** Compare actual expenses to budget to identify categories over or under plan.

## Tips

- Operating expenses exclude cost of sales but include all other business costs
- Expense analysis by department helps assign accountability and control spending
- Regular monitoring of major expense categories (salaries, rent, utilities) helps prevent budget overruns
