# Cost of Sales Report

Analyze the direct costs of producing or delivering products and services, including materials, labor, and overhead allocated to sales.

## What you can do here

- View cost of sales by period, product line, or business unit
- See breakdown of material, labor, and overhead costs
- Compare cost of sales as a percentage of revenue
- Track cost trends and identify cost increases
- Analyze cost by product to identify high-cost items
- Filter by customer, region, or time period
- Export cost data for budgeting and improvement planning

## Common workflows

**To review cost structure:** Select a time period to see material, labor, and overhead breakdown and total COGS.

**To identify high-cost products:** Filter by product to see cost of goods for each item and analyze margin impact.

**To track cost trends:** Use the chart view to see cost movements over time and identify inflationary pressures or waste.

## Tips

- Cost of sales is the direct cost to produce or deliver; it excludes operating expenses like rent or administration
- Analyzing cost by product helps identify which items are efficient to produce and which need process improvement
- Cost reductions in COGS improve gross margin and profitability
