# Workspace

Configure your workspace settings, business profile, and preferences.

## What you can do here

- View and edit your business name, logo, and contact details
- Configure workspace-wide preferences (currency, timezone, date format)
- Manage subscription and billing information
- View workspace usage and user counts
- Set up integrations and connected services

## Common workflows

**To update your business profile:** Edit the fields in the Business Profile section and save your changes.

**To change workspace preferences:** Navigate to the Preferences section and update the desired settings.

## Tips

- Workspace settings apply to all users and locations within the organization
- Changes to currency or timezone affect how data is displayed but do not alter stored records
- Keep your business profile up to date — it may appear on receipts, invoices, and client-facing documents
