# Fiscal Periods

Manage your accounting periods (monthly, quarterly, or yearly) for reporting and transaction posting control.

## What you can do here

- View all fiscal periods in the calendar year with status (open, closed, locked)
- Open new periods at the start of each reporting cycle
- Close completed periods to prevent further posting
- Lock historical periods to protect records from accidental changes
- View period-end totals and reconciliation status
- Generate period-close reports and checklists
- Archive old periods to improve system performance

## Common workflows

**To open a new period:** Click New Period, select the start and end dates, and the system will create the new period for posting.

**To close a period:** Open the period and click Close Period after verifying all transactions are complete and reconciled.

**To lock a period:** Locked periods prevent any posting or editing; use this for historical periods that are final and audited.

## Tips

- Open periods allow posting and editing; closed periods are for review and reporting only
- Locking ensures historical data integrity and prevents accidental modifications
- Period-end checklists help ensure all reconciliations are complete before closing
