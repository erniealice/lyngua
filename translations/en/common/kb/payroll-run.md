# Payroll Runs

Create, review, and process payroll runs to pay employees for completed work periods.

## What you can do here

- View all payroll runs organized by period and status (draft, pending approval, processed, paid)
- Create new payroll runs for a selected pay period
- Add employees and adjust hours, salary, bonuses, or deductions as needed
- Review payroll calculations and tax withholdings
- Approve and submit payroll for processing
- Process payments (ACH transfer, check, direct deposit setup)
- View payroll run history and reprocess if needed

## Common workflows

**To create a payroll run:** Click New Payroll Run, select the pay period end date, and the system will populate employees based on active payroll settings.

**To adjust employee pay:** Edit the hours, salary, or add bonuses or deductions before final approval.

**To approve and process:** Review the payroll summary, verify tax and deduction calculations, then click Approve to move to processing.

## Tips

- Most payroll runs process bi-weekly or monthly; verify your pay period is correctly configured
- Adjusting hours or deductions in draft status does not affect prior runs
- Approved runs are locked to prevent accidental changes; reversals are used for corrections
