# Inventory

Track inventory items and monitor stock levels across locations.

## What you can do here

- View all inventory items and current stock quantities
- Search by item name, SKU, or serial number
- Add new inventory items and set initial stock levels
- Filter items by location, category, or stock status
- Identify low-stock and out-of-stock items at a glance

## Common workflows

**To add an inventory item:** Click Add Item in the toolbar and enter the item details and initial quantity.

**To check stock at a location:** Use the location filter to view inventory for a specific branch or warehouse.

## Tips

- Items with stock below the reorder threshold are flagged automatically
- Use serial number tracking for high-value or individually tracked assets
- Stock adjustments are logged as inventory transactions for audit purposes
