# Jobs

Manage work orders, jobs, and task-based activities.

## What you can do here

- View all jobs by status (pending, in-progress, completed, cancelled)
- Search by job reference, client name, or assigned team member
- Create new jobs and assign them to team members
- Track job progress through defined phases
- Filter by date, status, or assignment

## Common workflows

**To create a new job:** Click New Job in the toolbar, select the client and template, and assign team members.

**To update job status:** Open the job detail page and use the status action button to advance the workflow.

## Tips

- Job templates let you pre-define phases and tasks for recurring work types
- Use the list filters to focus on jobs that need attention
- Cancelled jobs are retained for record-keeping and can be referenced in reports
