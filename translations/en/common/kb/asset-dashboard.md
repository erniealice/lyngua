# Asset Overview

View a summary dashboard of all fixed assets, their current values, depreciation status, and key metrics.

## What you can do here

- See total asset count by status (active, inactive, disposed)
- View total gross asset value and accumulated depreciation
- See net book value of all active assets
- Review asset distribution by category and location
- Monitor depreciation by asset category and time period
- Access quick links to add new assets or view detailed asset lists
- Export asset summary for reporting or audit purposes

## Common workflows

**To view assets by category:** Click on a category card to drill into assets in that category and see their values.

**To check total book value:** The net book value widget shows total assets minus accumulated depreciation at a glance.

**To export asset data:** Use the Export button to download an asset list with acquisition cost, depreciation, and book value.

## Tips

- The asset overview provides a high-level view for management and financial reporting
- Comparing gross value to net book value helps assess the age and condition of your asset base
- Regular reviews help identify assets that may be obsolete or candidates for replacement
