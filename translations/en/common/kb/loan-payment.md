# Loan Payments

Track individual loan payments, including principal repayment, interest expense, and any fees or adjustments.

## What you can do here

- View all payments for a loan with date, amount, and breakdown of principal and interest
- Record new loan payments with payment date, method, and confirmation details
- Review payment history and schedule upcoming payments
- Edit or correct payment records before reconciliation
- Apply extra payments or balloon payments to the loan
- Export payment history for reporting and verification
- View impact on loan balance and interest calculation

## Common workflows

**To record a payment:** Open the related loan, click Add Payment, enter the payment date and amount, and the system will split it into principal and interest based on the amortization schedule.

**To make an extra payment:** Enter an amount exceeding the scheduled payment; the extra amount reduces principal and shortens the loan term.

**To verify payment accuracy:** Review the breakdown showing how much went to principal vs. interest to confirm correct application.

## Tips

- Principal reduction lowers the outstanding balance and future interest expense
- Interest-only payments maintain the principal balance but satisfy contractual obligations
- Early extra payments can save significant interest expense over the loan term
