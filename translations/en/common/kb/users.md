# Users

Manage system users and their access to the workspace.

## What you can do here

- View all users and their current status (active, invited, disabled)
- Search users by name or email
- Invite new users to the workspace
- Manage user roles and permissions
- Disable or reactivate user accounts

## Common workflows

**To invite a new user:** Click Add User in the toolbar, enter their email, and assign an initial role.

**To change a user's role:** Open the user detail page and update their role assignments.

## Tips

- Disabled users lose access immediately but their activity history is preserved
- Each user must have at least one role assigned to access the system
- Use the workspace user list to audit who has access to your organization
