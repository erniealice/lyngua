# Job Detail

View job phases, activities, materials, and settlement information.

## What you can do here

- View and update job information (client, dates, assigned team)
- Track phases and their completion status
- Log activities and time entries against the job
- Record materials and expenses used
- View the settlement breakdown (labor, materials, expenses)

## Common workflows

**To log an activity:** Select the Activities tab and click Add Activity to record work performed.

**To record materials:** Select the Materials tab and add items used for the job.

## Tips

- Completed jobs are locked — use the revision workflow for post-completion changes
- The settlement tab provides a full cost and revenue breakdown for the job
- Phase progress updates automatically as activities within each phase are completed
