# Reports

Access business reports for revenue, inventory, and gross profit analysis.

## What you can do here

- View revenue reports by date range, location, or product category
- Check inventory valuation and stock movement summaries
- Analyze gross profit margins across products and time periods
- Export report data for external analysis
- Compare performance across locations or periods

## Common workflows

**To generate a report:** Select the report type, set the date range and filters, then click Generate.

**To export data:** After generating a report, use the Export button to download the results.

## Tips

- Reports reflect completed transactions only — pending and voided items are excluded
- Use date range comparisons to identify trends and seasonal patterns
- Gross profit calculations depend on accurate cost data in your inventory records
