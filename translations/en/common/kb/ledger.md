# Chart of Accounts

Manage your general ledger chart of accounts, which is the complete listing of all accounts used to record financial transactions.

## What you can do here

- View all accounts organized by type (assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expense)
- Create new accounts with account number, name, type, and description
- Deactivate accounts that are no longer in use
- View account balance and recent transactions
- Configure parent and sub-accounts for hierarchical reporting
- Export the chart of accounts for audit or integration purposes
- Search accounts by name, number, or type

## Common workflows

**To add a new account:** Click Add Account, enter a unique account number, name, type, and description, then save.

**To organize accounts hierarchically:** Assign a parent account to group related sub-accounts (for example, group several expense sub-accounts under an "Operating Expenses" parent).

**To deactivate an account:** Open the account detail, click Deactivate, and confirm. The account remains in history but is hidden from transaction dropdowns.

## Tips

- Account numbers should follow a consistent numbering scheme for easy sorting and reporting
- Parent accounts roll up balances from all sub-accounts, providing a hierarchical view of your general ledger
- Deactivated accounts retain all posted transactions for historical accuracy and audit trails
