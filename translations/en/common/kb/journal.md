# Journal Entries

Create and manage manual general ledger journal entries to record adjustments, accruals, and other transactions not generated by automated processes.

## What you can do here

- View all journal entries organized by posting date and status (draft, posted, reversed)
- Create new journal entries with date, description, and debit/credit lines
- Search entries by date range, description, or related account
- Edit or reverse posted entries
- Attach supporting documents to journal entries
- Configure entry approval workflows
- View journal entry impact on account balances

## Common workflows

**To create a journal entry:** Click Add Journal Entry, set the posting date and description, then add debit and credit lines until balanced (total debits equal total credits).

**To post an entry:** Complete the entry, review balances, and click Post. Posting updates the general ledger immediately.

**To reverse an entry:** Open a posted entry and click Reverse to create an offsetting entry with opposite debit/credit amounts.

## Tips

- Journal entries must balance (total debits must equal total credits) before posting
- Use clear descriptions and account selections to ensure your journal entries are easy to audit
- Reversing entries preserves the history of both the original and reverse transaction
