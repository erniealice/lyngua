# Employee Payroll

Manage employee payroll records, compensation history, and pay-period details.

## What you can do here

- View all employees and their payroll information
- See payroll history including gross wages, deductions, and net pay
- View year-to-date earnings and tax withholdings
- Access individual pay stubs and earning statements
- Track salary changes and pay rate history
- View payroll deductions (tax, insurance, retirement contributions)
- Export pay history for employee requests or verification

## Common workflows

**To view an employee's pay history:** Open the employee record and select the Payroll tab to see all completed pay periods.

**To access a pay stub:** Select a specific pay period from the history to view the detailed breakdown of earnings, deductions, and net pay.

**To view year-to-date totals:** The YTD summary shows gross earnings, taxes withheld, and deductions for the current year.

## Tips

- Pay stubs serve as documentation for employees and can be provided for loan applications or verification
- Year-to-date amounts are used to prepare W-2 statements and tax filings
- Payroll history helps resolve pay inquiries and supports employee benefit administration
