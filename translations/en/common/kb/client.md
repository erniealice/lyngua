# Clients

Manage your client and customer records in one place.

## What you can do here

- View all clients by status (active, prospect, inactive)
- Search by name, email, or phone number
- Add new clients with contact and preference details
- Export client lists for reporting or outreach
- Tag clients with categories for filtering and segmentation

## Common workflows

**To add a new client:** Click the Add button in the toolbar and complete the contact form.

**To find a specific client:** Use the search bar or apply filters by status, tag, or date added.

## Tips

- Clients with no transactions yet appear as Prospects
- Use tags to organize clients into meaningful groups for follow-up
- Inactive clients are retained for historical reporting — they can be reactivated at any time
