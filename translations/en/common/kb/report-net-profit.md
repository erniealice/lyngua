# Net Profit Report

Analyze net profit (revenue minus all expenses) to see overall business profitability after all costs and expenses.

## What you can do here

- View net profit by period, segment, or business unit
- See net profit margin (net profit divided by revenue)
- Compare profitability across segments to identify profitable and struggling areas
- Track revenue, cost of sales, operating expenses, and taxes
- Identify periods of profitability and loss
- View year-to-date profit and progress toward targets
- Export profit data for strategic analysis and forecasting

## Common workflows

**To review profitability:** Select a time period to see total revenue, expenses, and resulting net profit.

**To analyze segment performance:** Filter by product line, business unit, or customer type to see which areas are most profitable.

**To compare to targets:** View actual profit against budget or forecast to assess performance against plan.

## Tips

- Net profit is the bottom-line profitability after all costs and operating expenses
- Net margin percentage shows what percentage of revenue becomes profit after all expenses
- Regular monitoring helps identify cost overruns and profitability issues early
