# Payroll Settings

Configure your payroll system, including pay periods, tax withholdings, deductions, and compliance settings.

## What you can do here

- Set up pay frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly)
- Configure federal, state, and local tax withholding rules
- Set up payroll deductions (health insurance, retirement, garnishments)
- Configure direct deposit and payment processing
- Define payroll accrual and expense posting rules
- Set remittance schedules for taxes and contributions
- Configure year-end reporting and form generation

## Common workflows

**To configure pay period:** Go to Pay Period Settings, select your frequency (bi-weekly, monthly, etc.), and set the start date of your pay cycle.

**To update tax settings:** In Tax Configuration, enter your federal EIN, state tax ID, and locality codes to ensure correct withholding calculations.

**To enable direct deposit:** Configure bank account details and payment processor integration so payroll can be processed electronically.

## Tips

- Pay period configuration should match your actual payroll processing schedule
- Tax withholding rules change annually; review and update settings before tax year changes
- Direct deposit setup simplifies payroll processing and improves employee satisfaction
