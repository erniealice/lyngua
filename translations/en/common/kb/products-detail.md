# Product Detail

View and manage a product's variants, options, and pricing.

## What you can do here

- View and edit product information (name, description, category)
- Manage product variants (sizes, colors, configurations)
- Configure product options and attributes
- Set or update pricing for the product and its variants
- View which price lists include this product

## Common workflows

**To add a variant:** Select the Variants tab and click Add Variant, then define the option values.

**To update pricing:** Edit the base price on the product detail or update prices within individual price lists.

## Tips

- Each variant can have its own SKU, price, and inventory tracking
- Product options define the axes of variation (e.g., Size, Color) — variants are the specific combinations
- Changes to the base product (name, description) apply across all variants
