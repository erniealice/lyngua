# Gross Profit Report

Analyze gross profit (revenue minus direct costs) to understand product and service profitability before operating expenses.

## What you can do here

- View gross profit by period, product line, or business unit
- See gross margin percentage (gross profit divided by revenue)
- Compare gross profit across segments to identify high and low margin areas
- Track cost of goods sold (COGS) and material costs by product
- Filter by customer, product, or time period
- View trend analysis and period-over-period comparisons
- Export gross profit data for detailed cost analysis

## Common workflows

**To analyze product profitability:** Filter by product line to see gross profit and margin for each product category.

**To review period performance:** Select a time period to see total gross profit and margin for that month or quarter.

**To compare segments:** Use the dimension selector to view gross profit by product, customer type, or other relevant segments.

## Tips

- Gross profit excludes operating expenses, focusing on production and delivery cost efficiency
- Gross margin percentage (margin divided by revenue) is a key metric for pricing strategy
- Products with low gross margin may need price adjustment or process improvement
