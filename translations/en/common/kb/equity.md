# Equity Accounts

Manage owner and shareholder equity accounts that represent the ownership interest in the business.

## What you can do here

- View all equity accounts organized by type (paid-in capital, retained earnings, distributions)
- Create new equity accounts for owner contributions or retained earnings
- Record owner contributions and investments
- Track distributions to owners (dividends, draws, withdrawals)
- View equity balance and changes over time
- Configure equity accounts for multi-owner or partnership structures
- View equity impact from net income allocation

## Common workflows

**To add equity capital:** Click Add Equity Account, select the owner and account type, then record the contribution amount and date.

**To record an owner distribution:** Open the equity account and click Add Distribution, enter the date and amount, and the system posts it to both equity and cash accounts.

**To view equity balance over time:** Select the Transactions tab to see all contributions, earnings, and distributions in chronological order.

## Tips

- Paid-in capital represents direct owner contributions
- Retained earnings accumulates net income not distributed to owners
- Owner distributions reduce equity and should be authorized per partnership or corporate agreements
