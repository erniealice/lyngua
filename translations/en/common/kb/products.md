# Products

Manage your product catalog, including items, services, and bundles.

## What you can do here

- View all products by status (active, draft, archived)
- Search by product name, SKU, or category
- Add new products with descriptions, pricing, and images
- Manage product variants and options
- Organize products into collections

## Common workflows

**To add a new product:** Click Add Product in the toolbar, fill in the details, and set the pricing.

**To archive a product:** Open the product detail page and use the Archive action.

## Tips

- Draft products are not visible in client-facing views until published
- Use variants to represent different sizes, colors, or configurations of the same product
- Products can belong to multiple collections for flexible organization
