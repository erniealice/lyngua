# Fixed Assets

Record and manage physical assets owned by your business, including equipment, furniture, vehicles, and other property.

## What you can do here

- View all fixed assets by status (active, inactive, disposed)
- Create new assets with acquisition cost, purchase date, and depreciation method
- Assign assets to locations or departments
- Track depreciation and book value over time
- Record asset maintenance and repairs
- Generate asset reports and depreciation schedules
- Dispose of assets and record gain or loss on disposal

## Common workflows

**To add a fixed asset:** Click Add Asset, enter the asset name, category, acquisition cost, purchase date, and select a depreciation method.

**To track maintenance:** Open the asset detail and add maintenance records with date, description, and cost.

**To record depreciation:** Depreciation is calculated automatically based on the chosen method; review and post monthly or quarterly.

## Tips

- Accurate asset records are essential for financial reporting and tax compliance
- Use consistent naming and tagging to easily find and group related assets
- Depreciation calculations follow standard methods (straight-line, declining balance) based on your configuration
