# Fulfillment Details

View and manage the complete details of a single fulfillment record, including items, delivery status, and tracking.

## What you can do here

- View fulfillment number, creation date, and related order
- See items included with quantity, description, and unit price
- Update delivery address and recipient information
- Track shipment with carrier, tracking number, and updates
- View estimated and actual delivery dates
- Add delivery notes and proof of delivery
- Update fulfillment status and confirm receipt
- View cost breakdown (shipping, taxes, adjustments)

## Common workflows

**To update tracking info:** Open the fulfillment and enter the carrier name and tracking number in the Shipping section.

**To mark as delivered:** Click Mark as Delivered, confirm the delivery date and recipient, and save.

**To adjust fulfillment items:** If items were damaged or need to be changed, click Edit Items and update quantities or remove lines.

## Tips

- Proof of delivery documentation (signature, photo, receipt) provides protection for disputes
- Delivery notes help your team and customers understand any special handling or requirements
- Fulfillment status updates automatically sync to the original order
