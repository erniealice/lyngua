# Price Lists

Manage price lists and control how products are priced.

## What you can do here

- View all price lists and their status (active, draft, archived)
- Create new price lists for different pricing strategies
- Set a price list as the default for new transactions
- Assign price lists to specific locations or client groups
- Compare pricing across multiple lists

## Common workflows

**To create a price list:** Click Add Price List in the toolbar, give it a name, and start adding products with prices.

**To activate a price list:** Open the price list detail and change its status to Active.

## Tips

- Only one price list can be the default — switching the default deactivates the previous one
- Draft price lists are not available for selection during transactions
- Use multiple price lists for wholesale, retail, promotional, or location-specific pricing
