# Equity Transactions

View and manage individual equity account transactions including contributions, earnings, and distributions.

## What you can do here

- View transaction date, amount, type (contribution, earnings, distribution), and description
- See the impact on owner equity balance
- View related journal entries that were posted for the transaction
- Record new equity contributions or distributions
- Attach supporting documents (contribution agreements, distribution authorizations)
- View transaction history and activity log
- Correct or reverse transactions if needed

## Common workflows

**To record a contribution:** Click Add Contribution, select the owner, enter the date and amount, and describe the contribution type.

**To record a distribution:** Click Add Distribution, enter the date, amount, and distribution method (cash, property, or other), and save.

**To attach authorization:** Upload or link the board resolution, partnership agreement, or other authorization document supporting the transaction.

## Tips

- Contributions increase owner equity and provide capital for business operations
- Distributions reduce equity; in partnerships and S-corps, they reduce the owner's basis
- Documentation of distributions is important for tax compliance and ownership agreement adherence
