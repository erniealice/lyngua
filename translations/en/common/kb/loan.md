# Loans & Financing

Record and manage business loans, lines of credit, and other debt financing used to fund operations and growth.

## What you can do here

- View all active and historical loans organized by status (active, paid-off, defaulted)
- Create new loan records with lender, amount, interest rate, and term
- Track loan repayment schedules and remaining balance
- Record loan payments and interest expense
- Configure automatic interest accrual
- View loan amortization schedule
- Update loan terms or make early payoff transactions

## Common workflows

**To add a new loan:** Click Add Loan, enter the lender name, principal amount, interest rate, term length, and start date.

**To record a loan payment:** Open the loan and click Record Payment, then enter the payment date, principal paid, and interest paid.

**To view amortization:** Open the loan detail and select the Schedule tab to see the complete repayment schedule with principal and interest breakdown.

## Tips

- Loan details (interest rate, term, lender) are used to calculate depreciation and interest expense
- Amortization schedules show how each payment is split between principal and interest
- Keeping accurate loan records supports compliance with lender requirements and tax reporting
