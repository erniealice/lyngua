# Fulfillment

Track and manage the delivery and fulfillment of orders from dispatch to final delivery.

## What you can do here

- View all fulfillment records by status (pending, in-transit, delivered, cancelled)
- Create fulfillment orders from sales or service orders
- Assign delivery addresses and routes
- Track delivery status and confirm receipt
- Update carrier, tracking number, and expected delivery date
- View fulfillment history for each order
- Generate packing slips and shipping labels

## Common workflows

**To create a fulfillment:** Open the related order and click Create Fulfillment, select items to include, and set the shipping address.

**To update delivery status:** Open the fulfillment record and select the current status (in-transit, out for delivery, delivered).

**To record delivery confirmation:** Once delivered, upload proof of delivery or mark as delivered and save the receipt timestamp.

## Tips

- Fulfillment tracking helps you monitor order progress and respond to customer inquiries
- Multiple fulfillments can be created for a single order if items ship separately
- Cancelling a fulfillment moves items back to pending fulfillment status for reshipment
