# Product Lines

Group and organize related products or services into logical product lines for easier management and reporting.

## What you can do here

- View all product lines organized by status (active, inactive, archived)
- Create new product lines with descriptions and hierarchies
- Assign products and services to product lines
- Set default pricing or margins for a product line
- View products and revenue grouped by product line
- Filter and search products within product lines

## Common workflows

**To create a new product line:** Click Add Product Line, enter a name and description, and optionally set default margin or tax settings.

**To assign products to a line:** Open the product line detail, click Add Products, and select items to include.

**To view product line performance:** Use the summary view to see total product count, revenue, and inventory by line.

## Tips

- Product lines make it easier to organize reporting and apply bulk settings to related items
- A product can belong to multiple product lines for flexible cross-categorization
- Inactive product lines are retained for historical reporting but are hidden from transaction dropdowns
