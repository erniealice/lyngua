# Revenue Report

Analyze revenue by period, segment, product line, or business unit to understand sales performance and trends.

## What you can do here

- View total revenue by period (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly)
- See revenue trends and growth rates over time
- Compare revenue across segments (product line, geography, customer type)
- Filter revenue by customer, region, or business unit
- View average transaction size and order count
- Export revenue data for further analysis
- Create custom revenue reports and dashboards

## Common workflows

**To review period revenue:** Select the desired time period from the filter to see total revenue and average transaction size.

**To compare segments:** Use the Segment dropdown to view revenue by product line, business unit, or other dimensions.

**To identify trends:** Switch to a chart view to see revenue patterns over time and identify seasonal or cyclical patterns.

## Tips

- Revenue reports help you understand which products, segments, or periods drive business results
- Comparing period-over-period growth helps assess business momentum
- Segment analysis reveals which areas need attention or investment
