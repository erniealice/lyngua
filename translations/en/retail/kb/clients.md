# Customers

Manage your customer records for retail transactions and loyalty tracking.

## What you can do here

- View active, prospect, and inactive customers
- Search by name, email, or phone number
- Add new customers with contact and purchase preferences
- View purchase history and lifetime spend per customer
- Tag customers with loyalty tiers or marketing segments

## Common workflows

**To register a new customer:** Click Add Customer in the toolbar and fill in the contact form.

**To view purchase history:** Open the customer detail page and select the Revenue tab.

## Tips

- Customers with no purchases yet appear as Prospects
- Use customer tags to target promotions and discount campaigns
- The lifetime spend figure on the detail page includes all completed transactions
