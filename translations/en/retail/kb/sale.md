# Sales Transactions

View and manage all retail sales transactions processed at your locations.

## What you can do here

- View all sales by status (completed, pending, voided)
- Search by reference number or customer name
- View itemized receipts and payment details
- Filter transactions by date range, amount, or status

## Common workflows

**To view a transaction:** Click the reference number to open the full receipt detail.

**To void a transaction:** Open the transaction detail and use the Void action (requires appropriate permissions).

## Tips

- Completed transactions cannot be edited — use a credit note or adjustment instead
- Reference numbers are generated automatically at point of sale
- Filter by location to view transactions for a specific store or branch
