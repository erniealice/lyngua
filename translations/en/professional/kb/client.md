# Clients

Manage corporate client relationships and track engagements across your professional practice.

## What you can do here

- Create and maintain a comprehensive client database with contact information and company details
- Track active engagements and matters across all clients
- View billing history and payment status for each client relationship
- Manage client preferences, communication methods, and designated contacts
- Archive inactive clients while maintaining historical records
- Assign team members to client accounts and engagements

## Common workflows

**To add a new client:** Navigate to the Clients section and click "Add Client". Enter the company name, primary contact information, and industry type. Assign an account lead or relationship manager.

**To view engagement history:** Open a client profile and select the "Engagements" tab to see all matters, projects, or service engagements with timelines and status.

**To update billing preferences:** Access the client's profile settings and configure billing contact, invoice delivery method, payment terms, and billing address.

**To manage client contacts:** From the client detail view, add or update multiple contacts with different roles (billing, legal, operations) for more effective communication.

**To track client communications:** Use the activity log on the client profile to record calls, meetings, emails, and important interactions with the client.

## Tips

- Set up billing preferences upfront to streamline invoicing and reduce payment delays
- Assign multiple team members to key accounts for better coverage and client relationship continuity
- Use client tags or categories to segment your practice (industry, size, engagement type) for easier reporting
- Regularly review client communication preferences to ensure compliance and maintain strong relationships
- Keep client contact information current as personnel changes occur to avoid service disruptions
