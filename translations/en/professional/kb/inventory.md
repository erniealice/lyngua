# Materials & Supplies

Physical materials and supplies used in professional service delivery.

## What you can do here

- Create and manage inventory records for office supplies, specialized equipment, and materials consumed during service delivery
- Track inventory levels, reorder points, and supplier information for physical materials
- Assign materials to cost centers or projects to allocate supply costs accurately
- Monitor material usage and consumption patterns to optimize procurement and control costs
- Set up alerts for low stock levels to ensure timely reordering and service continuity

## Common workflows

**To add a material to inventory:** Click "New Material", enter the material name, supplier, unit cost, and current stock level, then save.

**To record material usage:** Log material consumption when used in billable work, either during time entry or through direct inventory adjustment.

**To reorder materials:** Review low-stock alerts and create purchase orders or requisitions for materials approaching the reorder point.

**To adjust inventory:** Correct inventory counts after physical stock-taking or record damage/obsolescence by adjusting stock quantities.

**To track material costs:** Review material usage reports and cost allocation to projects to identify cost management opportunities.

## Tips

- Establish reorder points based on historical consumption rates and lead times from suppliers to maintain adequate stock
- Link frequently used materials to specific services or practice areas to simplify usage tracking and cost allocation
- Conduct periodic physical inventory counts to verify system records and catch discrepancies early
- Track material unit costs and update them regularly to reflect supplier price changes and negotiate better rates
- Consider batch purchasing of high-volume materials to achieve quantity discounts and reduce procurement overhead
