# Invoices

Track and manage invoices and billing records for professional services rendered to clients.

## What you can do here

- View all invoices and revenue records from past and current client work
- Track billable hours and time-based billing from client engagements
- Manage retainer agreements and recurring billing schedules
- Monitor payment status and outstanding balances
- Generate billing reports for analysis and client communications

## Common workflows

**To create an invoice:** Generate a new revenue record linked to a completed client engagement or billable time period.

**To track billable hours:** Log hours spent on client work and automatically generate invoice line items based on hourly rates.

## Tips

- Link all revenue records to specific client engagements for complete visibility into profitability by client and project.
- Track retainer balances regularly to ensure clients maintain sufficient prepaid amounts.
- Use billable hour logs to ensure all client work is properly captured and billed.
