# Rate Card Details

Individual rate card with per-service pricing for a specific client segment, engagement type, or billing arrangement.

## What you can do here

- View the complete rate card including name, effective date, and target client segment or engagement type
- Review all services assigned to this rate card and their specific billing rates
- Update service-level pricing to reflect negotiated rates, volume discounts, or tier-specific adjustments
- Monitor which clients and engagements are using this rate card for billing verification
- Compare this rate card against standard rates to identify pricing variations and discounts applied

## Common workflows

**To add a service to a rate card:** Click "Add Service", select the service name, enter the rate card-specific billing rate, and save.

**To adjust a service rate:** Edit the specific service billing rate within the rate card to reflect client negotiations, market conditions, or volume discounts.

**To view rate card usage:** Access the client and engagement list to see which relationships are using this rate card and their billing impact.

**To create a variant rate card:** Duplicate an existing rate card as a template for a similar client tier, then adjust specific service rates as needed.

**To retire a rate card:** Archive the rate card when it is no longer in use while maintaining all historical billing records for auditing purposes.

## Tips

- Document the rationale behind rate card discounts or premium pricing to support future rate negotiations and pricing strategy discussions
- Use consistent naming conventions that clearly indicate the rate card type (e.g., client name, tier level, engagement type) to avoid billing errors
- Regularly compare rate cards to identify outliers and ensure pricing is strategically aligned with client relationships
- Set effective dates clearly to avoid confusion when multiple versions of a rate card exist
- Review rate card performance periodically to determine if discounts are justified or if standard rates should be adjusted
