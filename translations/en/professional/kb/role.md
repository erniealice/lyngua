# Roles & Permissions

Define and manage professional hierarchy and system access levels for your practice team.

## What you can do here

- View all organizational roles and their permission sets (Partner, Associate, Consultant, Admin)
- Create custom roles tailored to your practice structure and workflow requirements
- Assign team members to roles to control their system access and approval authority
- Configure which features and data each role can access, modify, or approve
- Set up role-based workflows (e.g., partners must approve matter budgets)
- Review audit logs showing which roles performed specific actions in the system

## Common workflows

**To assign a team member to a role:** Go to Team Members, select the member, and click "Change Role". Choose the appropriate role (Partner, Associate, Consultant, Admin) and save.

**To create a custom role:** Navigate to Roles & Permissions and click "New Role". Enter a role name, select permissions from the available list (see clients, edit matters, approve invoices, etc.), then save.

**To view a role's permissions:** Open the Roles section and click on a role to see its complete permission set, including which features it can access and what actions it can perform.

**To modify a standard role:** Click on an existing role and update its permission set. Changes apply immediately to all team members with that role.

**To set approval requirements:** Configure which actions require role-based approval (e.g., matters over $50,000 require partner approval) in the workflow settings.

## Tips

- Use standard roles (Partner, Associate, Consultant, Admin) as much as possible to maintain consistency and simplify management
- Regularly audit role assignments to ensure team members have appropriate access levels for their current responsibilities
- Document any custom roles and their intended use cases so future staff understand the practice's permission structure
- Set up approval workflows for high-stakes decisions (significant matters, budget changes, rate adjustments) by role
- Review role permissions annually to ensure they still match your practice's evolving structure and compliance requirements
