# Engagements

Manage active client projects, engagements, and matters in professional services work.

## What you can do here

- Create and track new client engagements, matters, or projects with defined scope
- Assign staff members and track their responsibilities and time allocations
- Log billable hours and monitor productivity against engagement targets
- Track engagement milestones, deliverables, and completion status
- Monitor engagement profitability and budget utilization

## Common workflows

**To create an engagement:** Create a new engagement record linked to the client, define the scope of work, set the timeline, and assign team members.

**To track billable hours:** Log time entries against the engagement by task or service type to capture accurate billing data and monitor hours against budget.

## Tips

- Define clear engagement scope and budget at the start to manage client expectations and prevent scope creep.
- Assign a primary contact or engagement manager to coordinate communication and ensure accountability throughout the engagement.
- Regularly update engagement status and milestones to keep the client and team informed on progress.
