# Capital Transactions

Record and track individual capital account transactions including contributions, draws, and profit allocations.

## What you can do here

- Record partner capital contributions and investment amounts
- Register partner draws, withdrawals, and distribution transactions
- Allocate firm profits to individual partner accounts
- Track capital adjustments and revaluations for audit purposes
- View detailed transaction history for each partner

## Common workflows

**To record a capital contribution:** Enter the partner name, contribution amount, date, and transaction details to increase their capital account.

**To process a partner draw:** Record cash withdrawals, in-kind distributions, or loan repayments against a partner's capital account.

**To allocate period profits:** Distribute calculated period profits across partners based on ownership percentages or partnership agreement terms.

## Tips

- Keep contributions and draws separate in your records for clear capital account tracking
- Reconcile profit allocations monthly to ensure they match firm profitability calculations
- Document the basis for all capital adjustments for audit and tax compliance purposes
