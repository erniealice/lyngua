# Payroll Settings

Configure firm payroll settings — pay periods, tax rates, deduction types, and payment methods.

## What you can do here

- Define pay periods and payroll frequency (weekly, biweekly, monthly)
- Set up tax rates and withholding formulas by jurisdiction
- Create and manage deduction types (benefits, loans, garnishments)
- Configure payment methods (direct deposit, check, wire transfer)
- Maintain employer identification and regulatory information

## Common workflows

**To set up payroll periods:** Define your standard pay periods and establish automatic payroll schedules for the year.

**To configure tax withholdings:** Enter applicable tax rates, deduction formulas, and jurisdictional requirements for your firm.

**To add deduction types:** Create new deduction categories for benefits, loans, garnishments, or other employee withholdings.

## Tips

- Review payroll settings annually to incorporate new tax rates or regulatory changes
- Keep backup documentation of all tax rates and withholding formulas for compliance verification
- Test payroll settings with sample employees before committing to live payroll processing
