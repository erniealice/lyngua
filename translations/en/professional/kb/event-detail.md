# Appointment Details

View and manage an individual appointment with client details, attendees, preparation notes, and outcome recording.

## What you can do here

- View appointment date, time, location, and client or matter information
- Manage attendees and track who will participate in the appointment
- Add and review preparation notes, agendas, and supporting documents
- Record meeting outcomes, discussion points, and decisions made
- Document action items and assign follow-up tasks to team members

## Common workflows

**To add attendees:** Invite specific staff members and external participants to the appointment and track RSVPs.

**To prepare for the meeting:** Add preparation notes, attach supporting documents or case materials, and create an agenda for the discussion.

## Tips

- Prepare a clear agenda and share it with attendees before the meeting to ensure focused and productive discussion.
- Record key decisions and client requirements immediately after the meeting while details are fresh and complete.
- Assign action items to specific individuals with clear due dates to ensure accountability and timely follow-up.
