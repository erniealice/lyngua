# Billing Terms

Configure standard billing terms and payment arrangements for professional engagements.

## What you can do here

- Create billing term templates for common engagement structures (Net 30, Net 60, retainer-based)
- Define milestone-based billing arrangements for project-oriented engagements
- Set up recurring retainer billing with monthly or quarterly billing cycles
- Configure time-and-materials billing with hourly rates and billing intervals
- Establish payment terms for contingency or value-based arrangements
- Manage discounts, deposit requirements, and payment method preferences

## Common workflows

**To set up a retainer billing term:** Create a new term specifying the monthly or quarterly retainer amount, billing schedule, and any included hours or services. Define whether unused hours carry over to the next period.

**To configure milestone-based billing:** Define each project milestone with associated deliverables and the percentage of the total engagement fee due upon completion. Specify invoice timing and payment deadlines for each milestone.

**To apply standard Net payment terms:** Create a billing term with Net 30, Net 60, or custom day terms, specifying when invoices are due after issuance. Set late payment penalties and discount policies if applicable.

**To create a time-and-materials billing arrangement:** Set hourly rates for different partner and staff levels, establish weekly or monthly billing cycles, and define expense reimbursement policies and markup percentages.

## Tips

- Use standardized billing term names that match your firm's market and practice area conventions
- Review billing term utilization regularly to ensure terms reflect current engagement profitability targets
- Consider offering tiered retainer options to provide clients with flexibility and improve engagement stickiness
- Document payment terms clearly in engagement letters to avoid disputes and ensure prompt payment
- Set up automatic late payment reminders to minimize days sales outstanding and cash flow delays
