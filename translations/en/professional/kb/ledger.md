# Chart of Accounts

View and manage the firm's full chart of accounts organized by element: asset, liability, equity, revenue, and expense.

## What you can do here

- Create new GL accounts by category and account type
- Organize accounts by account number and section
- View account balances and activity
- Control account usage and posting permissions
- Archive inactive accounts
- Export the full chart of accounts

## Common workflows

**To add an account:** Create a new account with number, name, type, and category, then assign the account section.

**To organize accounts:** Use account numbering to group related accounts by asset class, expense type, or revenue stream.

**To restrict posting:** Set account permissions to limit which users can post to sensitive accounts like owner equity or loan accounts.

## Tips

- Use consistent account numbering conventions across the firm for easier lookups and reporting
- Maintain separate accounts for different service lines or practice areas to track profitability
- Review the chart quarterly to archive unused accounts and clean up obsolete entries
