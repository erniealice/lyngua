# Revenue Report

Analyze revenue breakdown by client, engagement type, partner, and period.

## What you can do here

- View total revenue by client or engagement for the period
- Break down revenue by engagement type (hourly, fixed-fee, retainer)
- Analyze revenue by responsible partner or manager
- Compare revenue across periods to track growth or decline
- Segment revenue by practice area or service line

## Common workflows

**To analyze client revenue:** Filter revenue by specific client to understand account performance and identify growth opportunities.

**To review engagement profitability:** Compare revenue by engagement type to understand which service models generate the most value.

**To track partner contribution:** View revenue attributed to each partner to evaluate performance and align compensation.

## Tips

- Review revenue reports monthly to track progress against firm targets
- Segment clients by revenue size to identify high-value accounts requiring additional focus
- Compare revenue by engagement type to inform service delivery and pricing strategies
