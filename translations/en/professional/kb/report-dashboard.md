# Financial Dashboard

View high-level financial KPIs for the firm — revenue, gross margin, outstanding receivables, and utilization.

## What you can do here

- Monitor firm revenue performance against targets and prior periods
- Track gross margin trends and margin by engagement or partner
- View outstanding receivables aging and collection status
- Measure staff utilization rates and billable hours
- Compare key metrics to benchmarks and historical performance

## Common workflows

**To review financial health:** Check the dashboard for revenue, margin, and cash position to identify trends or areas needing attention.

**To monitor utilization:** Track billable hours and utilization rates by staff member to optimize resource allocation and profitability.

**To identify collection issues:** Review outstanding receivables aging to prioritize follow-up on slow-paying clients.

## Tips

- Review the dashboard weekly to catch financial issues early
- Set target ranges for key metrics to quickly identify performance variances
- Compare dashboard metrics across periods to identify trends affecting firm profitability
