# Office Locations

Manage physical offices, branch locations, and practice sites across your professional services firm.

## What you can do here

- Create and maintain multiple office locations for your firm
- Configure office-specific details like address, phone, and managing partner
- Set default billing and practice settings for each location
- Assign team members and staff to specific offices
- Track office operating hours and availability
- Manage office-specific practice areas or specialties

## Common workflows

**To add a new office location:** Create a new location record with the office address, contact information, and the partner or managing principal responsible for that office. Specify which practice areas operate from that location.

**To update office contact information:** Edit the location details to reflect current phone numbers, email addresses, or mailing addresses. Changes will be reflected in client communications and billing documents.

**To assign staff to an office:** Set each team member's primary office location for scheduling, billing, and operational purposes. Multiple office assignments can be managed for professionals with split arrangements.

**To configure location-specific settings:** Establish default billing terms, client communication preferences, and practice area focus for each office to reflect local market conditions.

## Tips

- Use consistent naming conventions for office locations to ensure accurate reporting and scheduling
- Keep office contact information current to maintain professional communications and client service
- Consider setting a primary office location for firm-wide defaults while allowing office-specific overrides
- Review office utilization regularly to optimize staffing and resource allocation
- Document office-specific policies such as billing rates and engagement minimums for consistency
