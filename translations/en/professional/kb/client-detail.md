# Client Profile

View and manage comprehensive information for a single client including contact details, engagement history, and billing settings.

## What you can do here

- Review complete client contact information including primary and secondary contacts with their roles
- View all active and historical engagements, matters, or service projects with the client
- Monitor billing history, invoice status, and payment records for the client relationship
- Update client preferences, communication methods, and designated billing contacts
- Access activity logs and communication history with the client
- Manage team member assignments and access permissions for the client account

## Common workflows

**To update client contact information:** Click the "Edit" button in the Contact Information section and modify company name, address, phone, email, or website. Save changes to update the client record.

**To add a new contact person:** Navigate to the Contacts section and click "Add Contact". Enter the person's name, title, email, and phone number, then assign their role (billing, legal, operations, etc.).

**To review billing history:** Select the "Billing" tab to view all invoices, payments, and billing adjustments. Filter by date range or engagement to find specific transactions.

**To assign a team member to the client:** Open the Team section and click "Assign Member". Select a team member and define their role (account lead, project manager, billing contact) and access level.

**To document client interactions:** Open the Activity Log and click "Add Note" to record meetings, calls, or important communications with the client.

## Tips

- Create billing contacts separate from technical contacts to ensure invoices reach the right department
- Document any special billing arrangements or rate negotiations in the client notes for future reference
- Use the engagement timeline to identify cross-selling opportunities or services the client may need
- Review the activity log before client meetings to refresh your memory on recent communications and agreements
- Set client notification preferences to control how many automated messages they receive from your practice
