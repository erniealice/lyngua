# Service Categories

Groupings of related services organized by practice area or engagement type.

## What you can do here

- Create new service categories to organize offerings by practice area (e.g., "Tax Advisory", "Litigation", "IT Consulting")
- Assign services to appropriate categories for clearer portfolio organization and client communication
- View all services within a category to ensure comprehensive coverage of a practice area
- Manage category properties such as name, description, and internal codes for reporting
- Track revenue and utilization metrics by service category across all clients

## Common workflows

**To create a new service category:** Navigate to Service Categories, click "New Category", enter a category name and description, and save.

**To organize services into categories:** Assign existing services to relevant categories by editing each service and selecting the appropriate category from the dropdown.

**To review a practice area:** Open a service category to see all services within that practice, check billing rates, and identify any gaps in your offerings.

**To rename a category:** Edit the category name to reflect organizational changes or revised practice structure without affecting individual services.

**To track category performance:** View revenue and utilization reports by service category to identify your strongest and most underutilized practice areas.

## Tips

- Use service categories that mirror your firm's organizational structure and practice groups for easier management and reporting
- Keep category names clear and consistent to avoid confusion when assigning services or generating client documentation
- Regularly audit service categories to ensure all active services are assigned and archived services are removed
- Use category-level reporting to evaluate the profitability and strategic importance of each practice area
- Consider creating categories for service types (e.g., "Advisory", "Execution", "Training") in addition to practice areas for flexible reporting
