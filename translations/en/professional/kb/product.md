# Services

Billable services and professional offerings delivered to clients.

## What you can do here

- Create and manage billable services such as legal consultations, audit reviews, or strategy sessions
- Define service names, codes, and detailed descriptions for client invoicing
- Set service-level attributes including default billing rates and time estimates
- Organize services by category or service line for better portfolio management
- Track which services are active, archived, or in development

## Common workflows

**To add a new service:** Navigate to the Services section, click "New Service", enter the service name and description, assign it to a service category, and set the default billing rate.

**To organize related services:** Create or assign services to service categories (service lines) that group similar offerings together, such as "Tax Advisory" or "Litigation Services".

**To retire outdated services:** Archive services that are no longer offered by marking them as inactive while preserving historical billing and project data.

**To update service details:** Edit service descriptions, billing rates, or other attributes to reflect current offerings and pricing without affecting past invoices.

**To view service utilization:** Check which clients have been billed for each service and review aggregate billing volumes across your service portfolio.

## Tips

- Use consistent naming conventions for services to ensure clarity on client invoices and reporting
- Regularly review and update service descriptions to reflect your current expertise and market positioning
- Group related services into logical categories to simplify client communications and service selection during engagement setup
- Archive rather than delete services to maintain complete historical records for auditing and trend analysis
- Leverage service codes for internal reference to avoid confusion when similar service names exist across practice areas
