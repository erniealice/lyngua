# Financing

Track firm loans, lines of credit, and debt obligations including office lease financing and equipment loans.

## What you can do here

- Record new loans with terms, rates, and payment schedules
- Monitor loan balances and interest accruals
- Track loan payments and interest expense
- Manage lines of credit and draw activity
- View amortization schedules and prepayment options
- Generate debt schedules for financial statement footnotes

## Common workflows

**To add a loan:** Enter the lender, loan amount, interest rate, term, and payment frequency, then the system generates the amortization schedule.

**To record a loan payment:** Enter the payment date and amount; the system calculates principal and interest portions automatically.

**To accrue interest:** Create a recurring journal entry that accrues interest monthly for loans not paid on a monthly basis.

**To manage a line of credit:** Track available balance, draws, and repayments for lines of credit used for operating needs.

## Tips

- Establish a consistent loan numbering and naming convention for easy identification and reporting
- Review amortization schedules regularly to verify interest calculations and principal payments
- Create recurring entries to accrue interest monthly for better monthly profitability tracking
- Document the purpose of each loan (equipment, real estate, working capital) for reporting and covenant tracking
