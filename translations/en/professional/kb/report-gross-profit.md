# Gross Profit Report

Analyze firm profitability after direct costs (staff time, disbursements) by engagement or period.

## What you can do here

- Calculate gross profit by subtracting direct costs from revenue
- View gross margin percentage by engagement or client
- Analyze profitability trends across multiple periods
- Identify high-margin and low-margin engagements
- Break down costs between staff time, disbursements, and subcontractors

## Common workflows

**To evaluate engagement profitability:** Compare revenue and direct costs for specific engagements to assess pricing adequacy and resource efficiency.

**To identify underperforming work:** Review margins by engagement to spot any work with unexpectedly low profitability requiring pricing review.

**To analyze partner margins:** View gross profit contributions by partner to evaluate team and individual performance.

## Tips

- Monitor gross margin trends monthly to catch pricing or efficiency issues early
- Keep detailed time and cost records for accurate gross profit calculation
- Compare margins by engagement type to optimize service delivery pricing
