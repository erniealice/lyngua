# Service Details

Individual service record with billing rate, description, and operational status.

## What you can do here

- View comprehensive information for a specific billable service including name, code, and detailed description
- Set and manage the default billing rate and time estimate for this service
- Monitor the active or archived status of the service and toggle availability to clients
- Access the service category (service line) assignment and modify if needed
- Review historical billing activity and utilization metrics for this service

## Common workflows

**To update a service rate:** Click "Edit", modify the default billing rate to reflect current market conditions or revised cost structure, and save the changes.

**To archive a service:** Change the service status from "Active" to "Archived" to prevent new engagements from using this service while preserving existing billing records.

**To reactivate a service:** Change an archived service back to "Active" status to make it available again for new client engagements and projects.

**To add service details:** Expand the description field to include prerequisites, standard deliverables, typical duration, or other relevant context for this service.

**To view service revenue:** Access the metrics panel to see total billable hours or revenue generated by this service across all clients and time periods.

## Tips

- Update service rates regularly to align with market rates and internal cost recovery targets for your practice
- Use the time estimate field to help track project budgets and resource allocation during engagement planning
- Archive services that are no longer offered rather than deleting them to maintain complete audit trails
- Add clear, professional descriptions that help clients understand what the service includes and what to expect
- Review underutilized services periodically to identify portfolio gaps or discontinued practice areas
