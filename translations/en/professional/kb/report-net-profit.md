# Net Profit Report

Calculate firm profitability after all operating expenses — useful for partner distributions.

## What you can do here

- Calculate net profit by subtracting all expenses from gross profit
- View net margin percentage by period or engagement
- Break down expenses by category (overhead, marketing, office costs, travel)
- Compare net profit to budgets and historical performance
- Analyze profitability trends across multiple accounting periods

## Common workflows

**To evaluate firm profitability:** Calculate net profit to determine available funds for partner distributions and reinvestment.

**To analyze expense impact:** Review expense categories to identify cost reduction opportunities and improve net margin.

**To support partner distributions:** Use net profit calculations to determine amounts available for partner draws and bonuses.

## Tips

- Review net profit reports quarterly to assess overall firm health and financial performance
- Allocate controllable expenses to engagement teams to drive efficiency and accountability
- Compare net margin to industry benchmarks to evaluate firm financial performance
