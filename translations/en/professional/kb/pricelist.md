# Rate Cards

Billing rate structures and pricing schedules for different client tiers or engagement types.

## What you can do here

- Create and manage rate cards for different client segments, engagement types, or service tiers
- Define per-service billing rates that override default service rates for specific rate cards
- Track rate card assignments to clients and engagements for consistent pricing
- Monitor rate card utilization and revenue contribution across your client base
- Archive old rate cards while maintaining historical pricing data for past engagements

## Common workflows

**To create a new rate card:** Navigate to Rate Cards, click "New Rate Card", name it for the client tier or engagement type, and assign services with their specific billing rates.

**To apply a rate card to a client:** During client setup or engagement creation, select the appropriate rate card to establish the billing rate structure for that relationship.

**To update rates for a new period:** Create a new version of a rate card with updated pricing, effective from a specified date, while keeping the previous version for historical reference.

**To compare pricing across rate cards:** View side-by-side pricing for the same services across different rate cards to ensure consistent value positioning.

**To retire a rate card:** Archive a rate card that is no longer in use while preserving all historical billing data associated with that pricing structure.

## Tips

- Name rate cards descriptively (e.g., "Standard Hourly", "Retainer Clients", "NPO Discount") to clearly indicate their purpose and target audience
- Review rate cards regularly to ensure pricing aligns with current market rates and internal cost recovery objectives
- Create tiered rate cards for volume discounts or long-term partnerships to maintain competitiveness while protecting margins
- Document the effective date and any special terms for each rate card to avoid confusion during engagement setup
- Use rate card versioning to maintain an audit trail of pricing changes and support historical billing verification
