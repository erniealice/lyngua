# Cost of Services Report

Track direct costs of delivering services — staff hours, disbursements, and subcontractors.

## What you can do here

- View direct costs by engagement, client, or service line
- Break down costs between staff hours, disbursements, and subcontractors
- Compare costs against billing amounts to assess profitability
- Analyze cost trends across periods and engagements
- Track cost allocations to individual projects or clients

## Common workflows

**To analyze engagement costs:** Review staff hours, disbursements, and subcontractor costs for specific engagements to assess cost efficiency.

**To evaluate cost performance:** Compare actual costs against estimates or budgets to identify overages or efficiency opportunities.

**To assess service delivery profitability:** Calculate cost margins for different service types to optimize engagement pricing and delivery.

## Tips

- Track all time entries and disbursements against engagements for accurate cost reporting
- Review cost reports monthly to identify any unusually high costs requiring investigation
- Use cost data to refine project estimates and service delivery pricing models
