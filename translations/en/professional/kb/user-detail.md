# Team Member Profile

View and manage detailed professional information, billing rates, and engagement assignments for a specific team member.

## What you can do here

- Review professional credentials, licenses, certifications, and specializations
- Monitor active engagements and current workload across all clients
- Manage billing rates, hourly charges, and client-specific rate overrides
- Update contact information and professional details
- View time and billing history for the team member
- Control system access permissions and role assignments

## Common workflows

**To update professional information:** Click "Edit Profile" and modify the team member's name, email, phone, office location, specializations (e.g., corporate law, tax consulting), and any relevant credentials or licenses.

**To set billing rates:** Navigate to the "Billing" section and enter the standard hourly rate for this team member. Add client-specific overrides for any accounts with negotiated rates different from the standard.

**To view active engagements:** Select the "Engagements" tab to see all current matters, projects, or service engagements. Click any engagement to see hours logged, client assignment, and billing status.

**To check utilization and capacity:** Open the "Utilization" view to see hours tracked this period, target utilization percentage, and availability for new assignments.

**To manage role and permissions:** Click "Role Settings" and assign the team member to a specific organizational role (Partner, Associate, Consultant, Admin) to control their system access and approval authority.

## Tips

- Keep professional licenses and certifications current in the system for compliance and when responding to client credential requests
- Review utilization metrics monthly to prevent burnout and ensure billable hours align with client expectations
- Document specializations and practice areas so the right expertise is matched to each client matter
- Use rate overrides for high-value clients or special circumstances to maintain accurate profitability analysis
- Enable calendar integration so the team member's availability is visible when assigning them to new engagements
