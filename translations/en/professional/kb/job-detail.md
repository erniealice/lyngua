# Engagement Details

View and manage an individual client project or matter with milestones, assigned staff, and billable hours logged.

## What you can do here

- View engagement scope, timeline, budget, and client information
- Add and track project milestones and deliverables with due dates
- Manage assigned team members, their roles, and time allocations
- View all billable hours logged against the engagement by staff member
- Track engagement status, profitability, and budget utilization

## Common workflows

**To assign staff to the engagement:** Add team members, specify their role or responsibilities, and set their time allocation or hourly rate for billing.

**To log billable hours:** Record time entries for work performed, categorize by task or service type, and update the total hours and billable revenue.

## Tips

- Update the engagement status regularly to keep the client and team informed on progress and any changes.
- Log hours against the correct engagement daily or weekly to ensure accurate billing and project profitability tracking.
- Set realistic milestones with clear definitions and responsible parties to manage client expectations.
