# Asset Details

View and manage an individual asset record with acquisition cost, book value, and depreciation schedule.

## What you can do here

- View asset acquisition cost, purchase date, and location
- Monitor current book value and accumulated depreciation
- Review the depreciation schedule month by month
- Adjust depreciation method or useful life
- Record asset disposal or retirement
- Add notes and attachments to the asset record

## Common workflows

**To update an asset:** Edit the asset name, category, cost, or depreciation settings, then save changes.

**To view depreciation:** Check the schedule tab to see monthly depreciation amounts and projected salvage value.

**To dispose of an asset:** Mark the asset as disposed and record the disposal date and any gain or loss.

## Tips

- Review asset details when preparing for period-end closes to verify depreciation calculations
- Attach purchase receipts or invoices to the asset record for audit trail purposes
- Update asset location when items are moved between departments or offices
