# Payroll Remittances

Track government remittances (tax withholdings, social contributions) arising from payroll.

## What you can do here

- View calculated tax withholdings and social contribution obligations
- Track remittance schedules and due dates for each liability
- Record remittance payments to government agencies
- Reconcile remittances against payroll-generated liabilities
- Generate remittance reports for compliance filing

## Common workflows

**To view remittance obligations:** See all outstanding tax withholdings and social contributions due to government agencies by type and due date.

**To record a remittance payment:** Enter the payment amount, date, and remittance type to track payments against calculated obligations.

**To reconcile remittances:** Match recorded remittance payments against payroll-generated liabilities to ensure all obligations are met.

## Tips

- Set up calendar reminders for all remittance due dates to avoid late payment penalties
- Keep supporting payroll documentation for all remittance filings for audit and verification
- Review remittance calculations quarterly to identify any discrepancies or adjustment needs
