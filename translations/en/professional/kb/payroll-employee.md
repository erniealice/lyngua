# Staff Payroll

Manage employee payroll records for professional staff, tracking salaries, billing rates, and payroll history.

## What you can do here

- Create and maintain employee payroll profiles and compensation records
- Set salary levels, pay frequencies, and employment status
- Manage billing and cost rates for time tracking and engagement costing
- Track payroll history, pay statements, and compensation changes
- Monitor tax withholdings, deductions, and employee benefits

## Common workflows

**To set up a new employee:** Create a payroll profile with salary, pay frequency, billing rate, tax information, and benefit elections.

**To update billing rates:** Adjust employee billing and cost rates to reflect compensation changes and ensure accurate engagement margin calculation.

**To review payroll history:** View historical pay statements, adjustments, and compensation records for any employee to verify accuracy.

## Tips

- Keep billing rates and cost rates separate to properly calculate engagement profitability and margins
- Update payroll profiles immediately when salary or employment status changes
- Review and reconcile all payroll records before submitting payroll runs to staff
