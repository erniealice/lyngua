# Loan Payments

Track scheduled and actual payments against firm financing and debt.

## What you can do here

- Record loan payment transactions against specific debt facilities
- Track payment schedules and due dates for upcoming obligations
- Compare actual payments to scheduled amounts for variance analysis
- View payment history and aging of outstanding debt balances
- Monitor principal and interest components of each payment

## Common workflows

**To record a loan payment:** Enter the loan account, payment date, amount, and allocation between principal and interest components.

**To track payment schedules:** Set up scheduled payment expectations for each loan and compare actual payments against the schedule.

**To analyze debt service:** Review payment history and remaining balances to understand debt obligations and cash flow impact.

## Tips

- Keep loan agreements and payment terms documented for reference when recording payments
- Reconcile payment records regularly with bank statements to ensure accuracy
- Track both principal and interest separately for proper accounting treatment
- Monitor upcoming payment dates to ensure timely fund availability
