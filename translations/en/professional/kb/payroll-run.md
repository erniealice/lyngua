# Payroll Runs

Process payroll runs for all staff — reviewing, approving, and submitting payroll.

## What you can do here

- Generate payroll runs for scheduled pay periods
- Review and verify payroll amounts before final approval
- Adjust individual amounts or deductions as needed for special cases
- Approve and lock payroll runs to prevent further changes
- Submit payroll to payment processors or banks

## Common workflows

**To generate a payroll run:** Select the pay period and applicable employees to automatically calculate salaries, deductions, and taxes.

**To review payroll details:** Check individual employee amounts, deductions, and net pay before approving the run for processing.

**To submit payroll:** Lock the approved payroll run and submit it to your bank or payroll processor for payment execution.

## Tips

- Review payroll runs within 48 hours of generation to catch calculation errors or missing information
- Keep detailed records of all payroll approvals for audit and compliance purposes
- Verify pay period dates and employee status before generating to ensure accuracy
