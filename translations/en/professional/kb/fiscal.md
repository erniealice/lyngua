# Fiscal Periods

Manage accounting periods by opening, closing, and locking fiscal months and years.

## What you can do here

- Define and edit fiscal period dates
- Track open, closed, and locked status for each period
- Restrict or allow posting to closed periods
- Reconcile and sign off on period-end adjustments
- Archive historical period data
- View period activity and transaction counts

## Common workflows

**To open a new period:** Set the period start and end dates, confirm the prior period is closed, then activate the new period for posting.

**To close a period:** Verify all transactions are recorded, run reconciliations, post closing entries, then mark the period as closed.

**To lock a period:** After closing is complete and approved, lock the period to prevent accidental edits or transaction deletions.

**To reopen a period:** Temporarily unlock a closed period if corrections are needed, then relock after changes are made.

## Tips

- Close periods promptly after month-end to create an audit trail and prevent late postings to prior periods
- Lock periods immediately after close approval to prevent unauthorized changes
- Maintain a close checklist to ensure all month-end procedures are completed before locking the period
- Document reasons for reopening any closed period for audit purposes
