# Practice Settings

Configure firm-wide settings including branding, professional credentials, and default engagement parameters.

## What you can do here

- Set your firm name, logo, and brand colors for client communications and documents
- Add and verify professional licenses and certifications (bar number, CPA license, engineering license, etc.)
- Configure default billing rates and billing terms for new engagements
- Establish firm-wide policies for engagement minimums, retainer structures, and payment terms
- Manage professional liability insurance and compliance documentation
- Set up firm contact information, website, and social media profiles

## Common workflows

**To update firm branding:** Upload your firm logo and configure brand colors that will appear in all client-facing communications, invoices, and proposals. Set the default practice area descriptions that represent your firm's focus.

**To add professional licenses:** Register your firm's professional licenses, certifications, and credentials with expiration dates and verification status. This information appears in client proposals and ensures regulatory compliance.

**To configure default billing settings:** Establish the standard billing term, payment method preferences, and billing cycle for new engagements. These defaults can be overridden for specific clients or engagements.

**To manage firm policies:** Document and configure firm-wide rules for engagement acceptance, minimum engagement fees, scope creep controls, and client relationship management standards.

## Tips

- Keep professional license information current and ensure all credentials are valid and in good standing
- Review and update firm branding at least annually to maintain consistency across all client communications
- Establish clear billing policies upfront to set client expectations and reduce scope creep disputes
- Use firm-wide default settings as a baseline, but allow flexibility for specialty practices or market-specific arrangements
- Document all firm policies in your engagement letter template to ensure consistent client communication
