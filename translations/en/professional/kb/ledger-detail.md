# Account Details

View details for an individual GL account including balance history, journal entry references, and reconciliation status.

## What you can do here

- Review account properties and posting permissions
- View month-by-month balance history
- See all journal entries posted to the account
- Track reconciliation status and notes
- Review account aging for receivable and payable accounts
- Drill down to transaction details

## Common workflows

**To verify an account balance:** Check the balance history to see the opening balance, all changes, and the current balance.

**To find a specific entry:** Search the journal entry list to locate and review the transaction details and supporting documents.

**To reconcile an account:** Compare the GL balance to external statements and mark reconciliation complete with supporting notes.

## Tips

- Reconcile accounts monthly during the close process to catch errors early
- Add reconciliation notes to document any timing differences between GL and external statements
- Review the transaction list regularly to identify unusual or unsupported postings that may need correction
