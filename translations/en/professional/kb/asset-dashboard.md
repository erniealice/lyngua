# Asset Overview

Dashboard showing total asset value, depreciation status, and asset categories at a glance.

## What you can do here

- View total asset value across the firm
- Monitor total accumulated depreciation
- See asset distribution by category and department
- Track assets by depreciation status
- Compare book values and identify fully depreciated assets
- Export asset summary reports

## Common workflows

**To assess firm asset value:** Check the dashboard to see total gross asset value and current book value.

**To review depreciation:** View the depreciation summary by category to monitor expense recognition by period.

**To analyze asset composition:** Use category charts to understand what types of assets the firm owns and their relative values.

## Tips

- Review the asset dashboard quarterly before period close to ensure depreciation schedules are current
- Use the category breakdown to allocate assets to cost centers and validate fixed asset schedules
- Monitor fully depreciated assets to plan for replacement or upgrade cycles
