# Partner Capital Accounts

Manage equity accounts for partners, shareholders, or firm owners showing capital contributions and draws.

## What you can do here

- View and track partner capital account balances and contributions
- Monitor profit allocations and distributions across partners
- Review capital account statements by period and partner
- Manage ownership percentages and capital requirements for partners
- Analyze capital account changes over time for planning purposes

## Common workflows

**To check a partner's account balance:** View the current account statement showing contributions, draws, and allocated profits for any partner.

**To allocate partner profits:** Distribute firm profits across partners based on ownership percentages or partnership agreement terms.

**To review ownership structure:** Monitor partner capital accounts and ownership stakes to understand firm equity distribution.

## Tips

- Maintain detailed capital account records for partnership tax and legal compliance
- Review capital accounts quarterly to track partner equity changes
- Consider capital account balances when planning partner distributions or capital calls
