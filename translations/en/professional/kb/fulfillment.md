# Deliverables

Track deliverables promised and delivered to clients including reports, filings, documents, and professional work products.

## What you can do here

- Create and track deliverable commitments linked to client engagements
- Set and monitor deliverable due dates and completion status
- Assign staff members responsible for preparing and delivering work products
- Manage document reviews and internal approvals before client delivery
- Track client sign-off, acceptance, and completion records

## Common workflows

**To create a deliverable:** Create a new deliverable record linked to the engagement, specify the type and description, set the due date, and assign responsibility.

**To track status and approvals:** Update deliverable status as work progresses and route for internal review before sending to the client for sign-off.

## Tips

- Specify deliverables clearly in the engagement scope and timeline to set proper client expectations and prevent misunderstandings.
- Set reasonable due dates that account for internal review and revision cycles before the final client deadline.
- Assign clear ownership to a single responsible party to ensure accountability and coordinated communication with the client.
