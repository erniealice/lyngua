# Schedule

Manage and schedule appointments, consultations, hearings, and professional meetings on the calendar.

## What you can do here

- Schedule client consultations, meetings, and appointments with dates, times, and locations
- Block time for court hearings, depositions, regulatory meetings, and other events
- Invite staff members, clients, and external attendees to events
- Set reminders and notifications for upcoming appointments and meetings
- Track event outcomes, discussion points, and follow-up actions

## Common workflows

**To schedule an appointment:** Create a calendar event with the client name, date, time, location, and purpose, then invite relevant team members.

**To prepare for a meeting:** Add preparation notes, attach supporting documents or case materials, and set reminders to review before the event.

## Tips

- Schedule internal preparation time before important client meetings to ensure thorough review of materials and strategy.
- Send meeting invitations well in advance to allow attendees time to prepare and arrange their schedules.
- Include a clear agenda and objectives in the event description to keep discussions focused and productive.
