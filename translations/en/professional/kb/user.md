# Team Members

Manage professional staff across your practice including partners, associates, consultants, and administrative personnel.

## What you can do here

- Invite and onboard new team members with appropriate roles and permission levels
- View active team members and their professional information (credentials, specializations, contact details)
- Assign team members to client accounts and specific engagements
- Manage billing rates, utilization targets, and capacity planning
- Track professional development, certifications, and continuing education requirements
- Monitor team member availability and current workload across active matters

## Common workflows

**To add a new team member:** Navigate to Team Members and click "Invite Member". Enter their name, email, and professional role (Partner, Associate, Consultant, Admin). Configure their billing rate and permission level.

**To assign a member to a client:** Open the client account and select the "Team" section, then click "Add Member". Choose the team member and define their role and responsibilities for that client relationship.

**To update billing rates:** Access the team member's profile and navigate to "Billing Settings". Enter the hourly rate, billing cap (if applicable), and any client-specific rate overrides.

**To view current workload:** Open a team member's profile to see active engagements, hours logged this period, and capacity utilization compared to targets.

**To configure role-based permissions:** Go to a team member's profile settings and assign them to a specific role (Partner, Associate, Consultant, Admin) which determines their system access and approval authority.

## Tips

- Set clear billing rates upfront to ensure accurate matter profitability tracking and client billing
- Regularly review team utilization to identify overloaded members and rebalance workloads accordingly
- Use team member specializations (tax, litigation, corporate, etc.) when assigning staff to client matters
- Document professional certifications and license numbers in team member profiles for compliance and client requests
- Configure escalation paths so team members know who to contact for approvals on specific decisions
