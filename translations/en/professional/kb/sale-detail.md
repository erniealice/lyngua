# Invoice

View and manage an individual invoice with line items, payment status, and client engagement reference.

## What you can do here

- View all line items and charges on the invoice with hourly rates or fixed fees
- Check payment status, amount paid, and outstanding balance
- Link the invoice to the specific client engagement or matter
- Record and track payment receipts and reconciliation
- Add notes, references, or attachments for accounting and client records

## Common workflows

**To record a payment:** Mark the invoice as partially or fully paid, record the payment method and date, and reconcile with received funds.

**To link to an engagement:** Associate the invoice with the specific client matter or project to track revenue per engagement and measure profitability.

## Tips

- Link every invoice to a specific client engagement for accurate project profitability tracking and billing analysis.
- Keep line item descriptions clear and detailed so clients understand exactly what services and hours they are being charged for.
- Review and verify all line items and rates before marking an invoice as final to prevent billing errors and disputes.
