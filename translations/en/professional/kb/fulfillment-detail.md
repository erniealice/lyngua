# Deliverable Details

View and manage an individual deliverable with due date, assigned staff, and client sign-off status.

## What you can do here

- View deliverable description, type, and client engagement reference
- Check deliverable status and due date with days remaining
- View assigned staff member responsible for completion
- Track internal approvals and review status
- Record and track client sign-off and acceptance date

## Common workflows

**To assign and track progress:** Assign a team member to the deliverable, set internal checkpoints, and update status as work progresses toward completion.

**To manage client approval:** Route the completed deliverable for client review, record feedback, and obtain sign-off to confirm acceptance.

## Tips

- Set internal review deadlines that are earlier than the client deadline to allow time for revisions and quality checks.
- Communicate clearly with assigned staff about expectations, requirements, and timeline to ensure timely and quality delivery.
- Obtain client sign-off in writing to create a clear record of delivery and acceptance for compliance and audit purposes.
