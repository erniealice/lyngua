# Reports & Analytics

View performance reports and analytics for your professional services firm's operations and profitability.

## What you can do here

- Track partner and staff utilization rates by time period and practice area
- Generate revenue reports by partner, client, practice area, and engagement type
- Analyze engagement profitability with margin calculations and cost tracking
- Compare actual hours and revenue against budgeted engagement amounts
- Monitor billing realization rates and collection performance
- Export reports for partner distributions and firm performance reviews

## Common workflows

**To view partner utilization:** Generate a utilization report showing billable hours, billable percentages, and realization rates for each partner over the selected period. Compare against firm targets to identify productivity gaps.

**To analyze engagement profitability:** Create a report by engagement showing estimated profit margin, actual costs, and profitability variance. Identify high-margin and low-margin engagements to inform future pricing and client management decisions.

**To track revenue by practice area:** Generate a revenue report grouped by practice area showing total billed revenue, average engagement size, and year-over-year growth. Use this to identify growth opportunities and resource allocation priorities.

**To review client billing and collection:** Generate a report showing billed revenue, amounts collected, days sales outstanding, and aging of unbilled time by client. Monitor collection patterns to identify problem accounts and forecast cash flow.

## Tips

- Run utilization reports monthly to identify staffing gaps and professional development opportunities early
- Compare engagement profitability against engagement budget to improve future estimating accuracy and pricing
- Track key performance metrics like realization rate, utilization rate, and average revenue per partner to benchmark firm performance
- Review practice area revenue trends quarterly to guide strategic investments in hiring, marketing, and client development
- Export reports in standard formats for partner meetings and firm financial planning to support data-driven decision making
