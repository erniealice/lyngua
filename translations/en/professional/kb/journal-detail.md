# Journal Entry Details

View and manage an individual journal entry with debit and credit lines, memo, and approval status.

## What you can do here

- View all debit and credit line items for the entry
- Review entry memo and business purpose
- Check approval status and approver history
- Add or remove line items
- Attach supporting documents or explanations
- Reverse or modify the entry if needed
- View accounts affected by the entry

## Common workflows

**To review an entry:** Open the entry to see all line items, verify the entry is balanced, and check the approval status.

**To approve an entry:** Review the line items and supporting details, then approve or request changes.

**To modify an entry:** Edit line items or amounts before posting, or reverse a posted entry and create a corrected version.

## Tips

- Attach invoices, contracts, or other supporting documents to entries for audit purposes
- Add detailed notes when entries are unusual or involve judgment or estimates
- Review approval history to understand who validated the entry before posting
