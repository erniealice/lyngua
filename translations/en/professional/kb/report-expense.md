# Expense Report

Track operating expenses by category — overhead, marketing, travel, office costs.

## What you can do here

- View total expenses by category for the period
- Analyze expense trends across departments or cost centers
- Track fixed versus variable operating expenses
- Compare actual expenses against budgets and targets
- Segment expenses by responsibility center or business unit

## Common workflows

**To review overhead costs:** Break down fixed operating expenses to understand cost structure and identify control opportunities.

**To analyze discretionary spending:** Monitor marketing, travel, and professional development expenses for budget compliance.

**To evaluate expense efficiency:** Compare expense rates as a percentage of revenue to assess operational efficiency and cost management.

## Tips

- Review expense reports monthly to identify unexpected increases or unusual spending patterns
- Assign expense approvers by category to ensure accountability and spending control
- Compare expenses to prior periods and budgets to track cost management performance
