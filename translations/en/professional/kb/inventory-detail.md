# Material Details

Individual material or supply record with stock levels, supplier information, and usage tracking.

## What you can do here

- View complete information for a specific material including name, SKU, supplier, and unit cost
- Monitor current stock level, reorder point, and consumption history for this material
- Edit supplier information and update unit costs to reflect current pricing
- Adjust inventory quantities for usage, returns, or corrections
- Review consumption trends and usage reports to optimize ordering and reduce waste

## Common workflows

**To update material cost:** Click "Edit", change the unit cost to reflect new supplier pricing, and save to update future cost allocations.

**To adjust stock level:** Record inventory adjustments for material usage, damage, obsolescence, or count corrections by entering the adjustment quantity.

**To change reorder point:** Modify the reorder threshold based on updated consumption patterns or lead time changes from your supplier.

**To view consumption history:** Access the usage log to see which projects or time periods consumed this material and identify usage trends.

**To mark material obsolete:** Archive the material record to prevent future selection while maintaining historical consumption data for reporting.

## Tips

- Update unit costs whenever supplier pricing changes to ensure accurate project cost tracking and profitability analysis
- Monitor consumption trends to identify usage patterns that may indicate inefficiencies or opportunities for process improvements
- Keep supplier contact information current to facilitate quick reordering when stock levels drop
- Set reorder points conservatively to avoid running out of essential materials during busy project periods
- Regularly review archived materials to determine if they should be reactivated as client needs or service offerings change
