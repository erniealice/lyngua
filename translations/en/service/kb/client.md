# Clients

Manage your client records for service bookings, appointments, and history.

## What you can do here

- View active, prospect, and inactive clients
- Search by name, email, or phone number
- Add new clients with contact and preference details
- View appointment history and service notes per client
- Tag clients with categories (VIP, recurring, referral, etc.)

## Common workflows

**To add a new client:** Click Add Client in the toolbar and complete the intake form.

**To view service history:** Open the client detail page and select the Revenue tab.

## Tips

- Client notes and preferences are visible on the booking detail page
- Use client tags to filter your client list for targeted outreach
- Inactive clients are retained for historical reporting purposes
