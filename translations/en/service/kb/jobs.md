# Jobs & Work Orders

Manage service jobs, work orders, and appointment-based tasks for your team.

## What you can do here

- View all jobs by status (pending, in-progress, completed, cancelled)
- Search by job reference, client name, or assigned staff
- Create new jobs and assign them to team members
- Track job phases, activities, and materials

## Common workflows

**To create a new job:** Click New Job in the toolbar, select the client, template, and assigned staff.

**To update job status:** Open the job detail page and use the status action button.

## Tips

- Job templates let you pre-define phases and tasks for recurring service types
- The settlement tab shows labor, material, and expense breakdowns
- Completed jobs are locked — use the revision workflow for post-completion changes
